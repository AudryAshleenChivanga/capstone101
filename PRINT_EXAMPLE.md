## 🎯 **Key Design Improvements**

### **1. Color-Coded Status**
- **Positive (Red)**: Immediate visual alert
- **Negative (Green)**: Reassuring confirmation
- **Warning (Yellow)**: Requires attention

### **2. Clear Information Hierarchy**
- Status (largest, most prominent)
- Probability (specific number)
- Recommendations (actionable list)
- Next steps (important warnings)

### **3. Professional Print Format**
- Medical letterhead
- Clear sections
- Signature areas
- Legal disclaimers
- Reference numbers

### **4. Action Buttons**
- **Print**: One-click printing
- **Copy**: Quick clipboard copy
- **PDF**: Save as document
- **Save**: Store in database
