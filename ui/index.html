CORE CAPABILITIES

Comprehensive Clinical Decision Support

Our system provides end-to-end support for H. pylori management, from initial screening to treatment monitoring.

Intelligent Symptom Assessment

AI-driven risk stratification analyzes patient symptoms and clinical history to identify high-risk cases requiring further evaluation.

Enhanced Lab Screening

Random Forest model with 400 decision trees achieves 70.1% accuracy, 84.9% recall, and 0.738 AUC-ROC. Calibrated probabilities provide reliable clinical risk stratification.

Antibiotic Resistance Staging

Multi-class Random Forest achieves 90% accuracy and 93.3% F1 score for resistance staging. Integrates MIC values and genetic mutations (A2143G, A2144G) for precise treatment guidance.

Evidence-Based Treatment Plans

Generate personalized treatment protocols aligned with current guidelines, including eradication therapy, dosing, and follow-up recommendations.

Integrated Patient Management

Comprehensive case tracking, digital prescriptions, and automated follow-up scheduling streamline clinical workflows.

Clinical Analytics Dashboard

Real-time insights on case outcomes, treatment efficacy, and resistance trends support data-driven clinical decisions.

CLINICAL WORKFLOW

Three-Stage Decision Support

Our evidence-based workflow guides clinicians through diagnosis, staging, and treatment planning with AI-powered insights at each step.

1

Symptom Assessment

Input patient symptoms, medical history, and risk factors. AI analyzes data to stratify infection risk and recommend testing.

2

Laboratory Screening

Enter lab results including biomarkers and diagnostic tests. ML model calculates infection probability with high precision.

3

Resistance Staging

Analyze antibiotic resistance markers and mutations. System predicts resistance stage and recommends optimal therapy.

4

Treatment & Follow-up

Generate evidence-based prescriptions with dosing protocols. Schedule follow-up tests and monitor treatment outcomes.
